October 2026

Tidekettle is quietly making paper and paper art here on Bridge Street and continues to develop and evolve. With much reluctance, and for purely logistical reasons, I decided this summer to stop teaching regular courses at West Dean, but individual students are welcome to contact me here if wished.

In the coming months I'll be restocking the IRL shop and tidekettle.com

Some must conquer mountains

Bag them, stand on top, bestride.

Taunted by weather, they hunker, mutter, plot, and try again.

As clouds scud by below them, punch the air.

 

Some prefer the journey down, the little caves, the mossy stones.

They know the crust is thin and seek their refuge there,

Among the boulders and the ferns

Beneath the sheep-cropped grass with Mother Time.

 

Lucy Baxandall, 2024
